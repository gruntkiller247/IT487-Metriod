	We wanted to create small rooms where the player was used as a soccer ball. The player will be able to use Samus' ball form and become the ball for 1-2 enemies who try to score a goal.

	The custome mechanic is the ablitity for enemies to hit and kick Samus while she is in ball mode within certain rooms. They would apply a force and push Samus in addition to the player's standard movement.

	The new mechanic offers a fun minigame for the player to play. The mechanic also offers a reward to the player in a powerup if they are able to score.

	The new mechanic of Samus as a soccerball will be demonstrated when the player enters with their weapons being unusable, a goal being presented, and the player changing color to match a soccer ball.
